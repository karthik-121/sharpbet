FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Is sharpbet in beta?

Yes. This is the beta version. Today it handles profit boosts and bonus bets on the main markets (moneylines, spreads and totals) for the NFL, college football, MLB and major soccer leagues, plus re-solving a grid if prices move while you place it and checking your own calculator's math.

More is coming soon, including: saved conversations and a bet log, remembering your books and bankroll, boost-assisted bonus hedges, +EV bets with bankroll sizing, more sports and markets (like NHL 3-way lines and player props), and low-hold and arbitrage scanning. Expect rough edges, and tell us when something looks off.

Is the profit guaranteed?

No. A locked profit holds only if every bet is placed at exactly the listed odds and nothing is voided. Odds move quickly, games can be postponed, and books can void bets or restrict promos. That's why every slip shows when its prices were pulled, and tells you to place everything in one sitting and stop if any price differs.

Does sharpbet place bets for me?

No. It never places bets and never touches your accounts. It tells you exactly what to place (book, stake, odds, game), and you place each bet yourself in your sportsbook's app.

Where do the numbers come from?

From code, not from the AI. Live odds come from an odds data provider, and every stake, payout and locked profit is calculated by sharpbet's own math. The AI handles the conversation and explains the result. If it ever states a number that didn't come from the calculations, the app flags it.

How fresh are the odds?

They're pulled live when you ask, from a feed that updates about once a minute. Each slip shows the time its prices were pulled. If the app shows a different price when you go to place a bet, stop and ask sharpbet to re-run.

Which sportsbooks does it work with?

The major US books in the odds feed, such as FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM and BetRivers. Coverage depends on the feed and can change; if there are no prices for your book, sharpbet tells you instead of guessing.

How can a bonus bet lock profit at just one sportsbook?

A bonus bet's stake isn't returned, so longer odds are worth more. sharpbet splits your bonus across parlays that together cover every outcome of a few games, sized so each pays about the same. Exactly one ticket wins, whatever happens. With good prices that turns roughly 76–80% of the bonus into cash, with no money of your own.

Will my sportsbook limit my account?

It might. Many books' terms restrict betting both sides of a game or hedging promos, and heavy promo use can lead to limits. sharpbet shows this warning on every slip where it applies; how you use your promos is your call.

Is it legal?

sharpbet is an information tool: it doesn't take bets or hold money. Sports betting laws vary by state, so only bet where it's legal for you, and you must be 21 or older. This isn't legal advice.
